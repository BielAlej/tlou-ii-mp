TLOU 2 MP Mod

v1.03 - Patch Notes

New Features

You can highlight/mark an enemy for your team if you aim at them and press the button that would normally toggle the flashlight. If you are on default controls that would be T on keyboard or R3 on controller Players are now based on the NPC class. As a result, there should be less crashes. Animation sync has been replaced with an entirely new system, almost identical to what Naughty Dog uses in Uncharted 4 Multiplayer and Factions. Previously we were using input based animation sync which had many inconsistencies. So we have 1:1 anim sync now. As a result of moving away from input based anim sync, this should get rid of crashes that stemmed from the input system. You can now heal, you are given 1 medkit on spawn. Spectate after death.

Team mates show on radar.

Voice lines now play when you start a match.

Kill feed added.

Fixes

Listen mode now shows enemies like it would in SP rather than an outline.

Shotguns fixed

Weapons

Most weapon stats have been adjusted to try and match the TTK, clip size and handling feeling of the original Factions. We're open to feedback and actively working on improving the viability of all weapons!

Long Firearm Nerf

Hunting Rifle

Two shots to down and pinpoint accurate at range.

Note

The Stitcher's fast TTK sometimes meant getting ambushed and killed before you could react. The high headshot multiplier was a large contributor — when close enough to land head shots, TTK numbers dropped well below other weapons. These changes bring headshot TTK closer to body shot TTK.

Pistol Rare Nerf

Venator

Dual-shot pistol still dominating PvP — second round of nerfs since 1.3.0.

Note

Since the last nerfs in 1.3.0, data shows the Venator is still one of the highest-performing weapons in the game. For a weapon of this tier it should perform well — but not this well. If the damage reduction doesn't work, further ammo/cost efficiency changes may follow.

Headshot Multiplier
2.5× → 2.0×
Base Damage
9 → 8
Further nerfs to ammo consumption and cost efficiency may follow if performance remains too high.
Sniper Rifle Legendary Buff

Jupiter

Highest headshot damage in-game — now more viable with better ADS and handling.

Note

The Jupiter does the most headshot damage in the game, but it's quite difficult to hit at range and missing a shot at close range makes you a sitting target. We're improving ADS magnification for better target acquisition and reducing equip/unequip times for faster weapon swaps.

ADS Magnification
~1.9× → ~2.2×
Equip Time
1.2s → 1.05s
Unequip Time
0.9s → 0.75s
Battle Rifle Legendary Buff

Aphelion

Legendary PvP rifle — now less bulky and more competitive.

Note

The Aphelion was intended to be a PvP-focused legendary weapon, but it couldn't keep up with the pacing of other PvP options. We've made it feel less bulky and more nimble to improve its PvP viability.

Base Reload Time
4.5s → 3.5s
Time Between Shots
0.9s → 0.7s
Vertical Recoil
~50% reduction
ADS Settle Speed
~35% faster
